# Changelog

Each release's notes are the section under its version, which the release workflow copies into the GitHub release and the updater shows before installing.

## Unreleased

- Supports the Arctis Nova 7X (`1038:2206`), showing its headset battery level.
- Supports the Arctis Nova Elite (`1038:2244`), showing its headset and spare battery levels as exact percentages.
- The window no longer shows a Dock line for headsets that have no spare battery, or while no headset is connected.

## 0.1.0

- Shows the Arctis Nova Pro Wireless headset and spare battery levels, read straight from the base station, without SteelSeries GG.
- Alt+Shift+B reads them from anywhere, through your screen reader or a system voice, and to a braille display. Change Hotkey picks another key, or none.
- Escape hides Headroom in the notification area. Click its icon, or launch it again, to bring it back.
- Checks for updates when it starts, and installs them once you agree.
